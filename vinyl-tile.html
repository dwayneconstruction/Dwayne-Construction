About Luxury Vinyl Tile

Luxury vinyl tile (LVT) gives you the look of stone, ceramic, or concrete in a floor that is waterproof and more comfortable underfoot than the real thing. You get the upscale tile look without cold, hard surfaces.

Why homeowners like it

  • Stone and tile looks
  • Waterproof and low-maintenance
  • Warmer and softer than ceramic

Is it right for you?

Great if…

  • You want a stone or tile look without the cold
  • You need a waterproof floor for a bath, kitchen, or laundry
  • You want an easy-care surface

Maybe not if…

  • You want real stone or ceramic tile
  • You prefer a wood look (see Luxury Vinyl Plank)
  • You would rather not prep an uneven subfloor

Where it works best in your home

  • Kitchens and laundry rooms
  • Bathrooms and powder rooms
  • Mudrooms and entryways
  • Sunrooms and living spaces
  • Any room where you want a tile or stone look

Installation notes

LVT installs like LVP, either click-together or glued down, over a clean, flat subfloor. Planning the layout helps the tiles line up cleanly across the room.

Care & maintenance

Sweep or vacuum regularly and mop with a pH-neutral vinyl cleaner. Avoid abrasive cleaners, wax, and steam mops. Wipe spills promptly.

Frequently asked questions

How is LVT different from LVP?

They are built the same way. LVP looks like wood planks, and LVT looks like stone, ceramic, or concrete, usually in square or rectangular tiles.

Is LVT cold like ceramic tile?

No. It feels warmer and softer underfoot than ceramic or porcelain.

Can LVT go in a bathroom?

Yes. It is waterproof, which makes it a popular choice for bathrooms, kitchens, and laundry rooms.
