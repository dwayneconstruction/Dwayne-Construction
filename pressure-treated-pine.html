About Pressure-Treated Pine Decking

Pressure-treated pine is the most widely used decking material and the budget-friendly starting point for most projects. It's real wood that's treated to resist rot and insects, and it can be stained in many colors.

Why Homeowners Choose It

  • The lowest upfront cost of any decking material
  • Treated to resist rot and insects
  • Real wood look that can be stained in many colors
  • Easy to build with, so projects move along quickly

Good For

  • Homeowners working with a tighter budget
  • Large decks, where material costs add up fast
  • Owners who don't mind regular maintenance
  • Decks you may want to restain a different color later

Lifespan & Upkeep

  • Lifespan: about 10 to 20 years with good care
  • Upkeep: clean once or twice a year, and seal or stain every 2 to 3 years
  • New boards: let them dry for several weeks before staining

Keep in Mind

  • Can warp, crack, and splinter as it dries and weathers
  • Turns gray if it isn't sealed regularly
  • Upkeep costs add up over the years
