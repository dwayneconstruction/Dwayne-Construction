About Cedar Decking

Cedar is a naturally beautiful wood with a warm color and a clean grain. It resists decay and insects on its own and feels comfortable underfoot, whether you finish it to keep its color or let it weather to a soft silver-gray.

Why Homeowners Choose It

  • Rich natural color and grain
  • Naturally resists decay and insects
  • Stays cooler on bare feet than composite
  • Can be finished to keep its color, or left to weather to a soft silver-gray

Good For

  • Homeowners who want the look of real wood and will care for it
  • Sunny decks where bare feet matter
  • Anyone who wants a more upscale natural look than pine

Lifespan & Upkeep

  • Lifespan: about 15 to 30 years depending on care and exposure
  • Upkeep: clean yearly, and refinish every couple of years to keep the color, or let it age gray
  • Cost: moderately more than pressure-treated pine

Keep in Mind

  • Softer wood, so it dents and scratches more easily
  • Can fade, check, and cup without finishing
  • Costs more than pressure-treated pine
